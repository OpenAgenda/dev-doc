# Typo3 Extension

The Typo3 extension for OpenAgenda allows you to integrate one or more agendas as white-label directly on your Typo3 sites version 10.3 or later.

Once installed, you can customize the style and templates of event lists, detail views, and filters.

Find the extension on [the Typo3 extension repository](https://extensions.typo3.org/extension/openagenda) or on [Github](https://github.com/OpenAgenda/typo3) where you will also find the getting started documentation for the extension.

A demo with a default presentation is available at <https://typo3.oagenda.com>
