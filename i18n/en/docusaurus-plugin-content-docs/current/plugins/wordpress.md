# WordPress Plugin

The OpenAgenda WordPress plugin allows you to easily integrate OpenAgenda events into your WordPress site. It is published on the official [wordpress.org](https://fr.wordpress.org/plugins/openagenda/) directory and is available under the MIT license on [github.com](https://github.com/OpenAgenda/wordpress).

To integrate your agenda into your WordPress site, follow the few steps detailed [in the plugin documentation](https://fr.wordpress.org/plugins/openagenda/).
